Introducing

Engineering Intelligence Hub

AI-Powered Tools for Engineering Excellence
 

Dear Team,

I am pleased to announce the launch of the Engineering Intelligence Hub — a unified platform of AI-powered tools designed to transform how we engineer, deliver, and lead across our organization.

This initiative is built on a simple belief: our engineering organization generates enormous amounts of data every day — sprint metrics, pull requests, bug reports, deployments — but that data has never been turned into real-time intelligence. Until now.

The hub connects directly to Azure DevOps and GitHub Copilot — tools we already use — and applies AI to surface insight that would otherwise require hours of manual effort. No new infrastructure. No behavior change from teams. The intelligence comes to them.

The AI Transformation

AI that reads your sprint data. AI that classifies your bugs. AI that writes executive summaries. AI that predicts delivery risk — before it becomes a problem.

Live Today

Four Tools Already Running

🐛 Bug Analyzer

Quality & Testing

AI classifies every bug, traces it to the originating commit, and generates root cause analysis automatically — turning hours of manual investigation into minutes.
 

📊 Executive Engineering Dashboard

Leadership Intelligence

Real-time health scores (Green/Amber/Red) for every team, with AI-generated executive summaries — replacing manual weekly status reports entirely.
 

🧠 AI Productivity Index

AI Value Measurement

A composite score correlating Copilot usage with real engineering outcomes — delivery, quality, velocity, and PR flow. The first data-driven answer to "Is AI making us more productive?"
 

📖 Story Extractor

Agile & Backlog

Claude Sonnet 4.5 reads mobile app source code and produces structured Agile user stories with acceptance criteria — work that takes days, done in minutes.
